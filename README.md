# 🏦 Banking Customer Churn & Retention Analytics Dashboard

## 🎯 Business Overview & Problem Statement
Customer retention is a critical challenge in retail banking. This Power BI dashboard analyzes a customer dataset of **10,000 clients** to identify key churn drivers, evaluate customer risk profiles across different geographical regions, and measure the impact of product engagement on churn rates.


## 🔑 Key Metrics & Insights
* **Total Customer Base:** 10,000 customers (5,151 active, 4,849 inactive).
* **Overall Churn Rate:** **20.37%** (2,037 churned customers).
* **Geographic Risk:** France holds the largest customer base, but Germany exhibits significantly higher churn behavior.
* **High-Risk Demographics:** Customers aged **46–60 exhibit the highest churn rate (51%)**.
* **Product Engagement Impact:** Single-product customers show noticeable churn, while 4-product holding customers face maximum attrition risk.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Business Intelligence Tool:** Power BI
* **Data Processing & Analytics:** Data Transformation, DAX Measures, Custom KPI Cards
* **Visualizations:** Donut Charts, Stacked Column Charts, Horizontal Bar Charts

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Target Inactive Customers:** Re-engage the **4,849 inactive customers** before they convert to full churn.
2. **Age-Specific Retention:** Formulate targeted financial products tailored for the 46–60 age bracket.
3. **Regional Focus:** Conduct localized market analysis in Germany to address elevated churn rates.
